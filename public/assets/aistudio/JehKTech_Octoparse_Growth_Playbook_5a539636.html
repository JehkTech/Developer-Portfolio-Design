01 · ThesisWhat "1000%" actually means

You will not work 10x harder. You get 10x by stacking three multipliers:

1. Fewer things

You run 7+ tracks at once. Output per track is capped by attention. One North Star per venture, a hard WIP limit, and a kill list gives roughly 2–3x on its own.

2. Automated inputs

Leads, market data, competitor intel, and content ideas should arrive on a schedule, not be hunted manually. This is where Octoparse earns its place: roughly 2–3x on research and prospecting time.

3. Compounding assets

Canonical domain, case studies, templates, datasets, the content clock. Every hour should leave something behind that works while you sleep. Roughly 2x on top.

Honest framing: 2.5 × 2.5 × 2 ≈ 12x on specific workflows, not on everything. The goal is to pick the 3–4 workflows where this applies and ignore the rest.

03 · ProjectsPlaybook per project

Luminous Pixels Agency · revenue engine

North Star: discovery calls booked per week.

  • Weekly lead machine: Octoparse job + Maps scrapes → sheet → Lead Scout agent scores 1–10 → top 20 get personalized outreach.
  • Start with SMBs (clinics, logistics, local services, property managers). Faster decisions than enterprise BPOs.
  • Productise one offer: "Hiring a receptionist/data-entry hire? Automate it first." One page, one price range, one demo.
  • Proof before pitch: run the automation on yourself (StatusHub intake, lead sorting) and publish the before/after.
  • Add the F6S agency profile and Contra case studies as trust assets.

R0TK Trading · capital engine

North Star: rule adherence %, not P&L.

  • Auto-brief: scrape economic calendar, EIA, headlines → Sunday "fundamentals for the week" sheet (playbook step one).
  • Copier risk limits at code level (1% / -1.5% daily / -3% weekly) already designed. Make breaches log to a journal automatically.
  • Journal analytics: win rate by session, setup, instrument, and time since last loss. Cut what loses, scale what works.
  • No-trade-day detector: high-impact news + no HTF alignment = a pre-written "no trade" that you honour.
  • Extend MNOG research with clean historical data (broker/Dukascopy exports; Octoparse is not the right tool for price feeds).

KTMNGW Content · audience engine

North Star: saves + shares per post, and email/Whop clicks.

  • One idea → 7 outputs (Reel, carousel, tweet, Reddit post, Discord drop, YouTube segment, Threads). Write the principle once, then adapt.
  • Question mining: Octoparse on Reddit/X for recurring trader questions; each recurring question is a post.
  • Batch weekly: one 3-hour block creates 14 days of content. Schedule everything.
  • Funnel: post → bio link → free R0TK checklist → Whop product → Calendly call. Measure each step.
  • Stay inside the brand filter: calm, no hype, no guarantees.

StatusHub · SaaS

North Star: activated accounts (first webhook or status page live in 24h).

  • Finish the core first: StatusPage CRUD, global search wiring, pg_trgm schema, SSE reconnect tests.
  • Competitor teardown: reviews + pricing of UptimeRobot, BetterStack, Statuspage → three differentiators.
  • Programmatic SEO: public status pages for Zambian/regional services ("Is Airtel Money down?" style pages).
  • Launch loop: "Powered by StatusHub" badge on free status pages = built-in virality.
  • Lead list: ISPs, fintechs, SaaS teams scraped from directories.

OnboardAI · SaaS

North Star: design-partner customers (target 3).

  • Validate before the 4-week backend sprint: find 10 agencies/SaaS teams via job posts for "client onboarding". Pitch the mock-data demo.
  • Get 3 verbal commitments or pre-orders, then build.
  • Dogfood it on Luminous Pixels client onboarding. It becomes a case study.
  • Mine competitor reviews for the top onboarding complaints; build only those fixes.

HRRAMS · ICU E-Governance · academic, deadline-driven

North Star: marks secured.

  • Tasks 1–2 are overdue. Task 3 (15 marks) is due 10 Oct. Treat this as P0 for the week and finish the recovery sprints first.
  • Reuse your own patterns (auth/RBAC, audit logging, case state machine) from StatusHub and Mamastops work.
  • Use only public, official sources if aggregating awareness content, and handle any report data with strong privacy by design.
  • Package it afterwards as a portfolio piece and a SaaS-style case study.

Career & Credibility · Mamastops, CV, research

  • Your strongest assets: five live payment integrations, 10+ shipped modules, a DOI-registered paper. Lead with those.
  • Scrape remote QA-automation / fintech UI roles weekly; track which skills and salaries repeat.
  • One proof-of-work post per month: "how we integrated X payment rail" or a test-automation write-up.
  • Keep the CV single-column, full-granularity, and address the no-degree point directly.

JehKTech Hub · compounding asset

North Star: your name + brand page-1 ownership.

  • Launch the canonical JehKTech domain: projects, research, writing, contact. Highest-leverage fix in this document.
  • Link all profiles (GitHub, LinkedIn, Zenodo, Academia, Contra, F6S) to it with consistent naming.
  • Add Person + Organization schema, a short bio, and one case study per product.
  • Track SERP position weekly with Octoparse.

Transport App Concept · parked idea

  • Do not build yet. First run a validation sprint: forum/social scraping on commuter complaints, 15 rider/driver interviews, a landing page with a waitlist.
  • Build only if the waitlist hits a threshold you set in advance (e.g. 300 sign-ups).

04 · ArchitectureThe Octoparse pipeline

Octoparse templates (cloud, scheduled)→Export / API / webhook→Sheet or Postgres→Automation (n8n / Make / script)→Claude scores & drafts→Notion board + outreach queue
  • Cadence: scrape Sunday night, review Monday morning, send Tuesday–Thursday (matches your own outreach guidance).
  • Dedupe by domain or place ID. Score with a fixed rubric. Never send without a human skim in the first 30 days.
  • Notion: r0tk teamspace for income work, ~work for client work, myVortex for personal. One database per pipeline.

05 · EfficiencyThe 12-lever operating system

Focus

  • WIP limit: max 2 active build projects + 1 revenue activity per week.
  • Rank by cash and deadline: HRRAMS marks, then agency revenue, then StatusHub, then OnboardAI.
  • Kill list: anything untouched for 30 days moves to "parked" with a restart condition.
  • One North Star per venture (section 03). Review weekly.

Time

  • Daily rhythm: deep work in your peak window, shallow tasks later, trading only in your set sessions.
  • Themed days: Mon sales, Tue–Wed build, Thu content, Fri review + ops.
  • Batch: content weekly, outreach in two daily blocks, admin once a day.
  • Your planning cycles stay as the scaffold. Give each 52-day period one outcome per venture.

Leverage

  • Reusable prompts and agents: Lead Scout, Copywriter, Reality Checker, Outreach Manager already exist. Wire them to the pipeline.
  • Boilerplate repo (Next.js 14 + Prisma + auth + Stripe-free payments) so each new SaaS starts at day 3, not day 0.
  • Claude Code for implementation; you write specs and acceptance criteria.
  • Component library from your 10+ shipped UI modules.

Measurement

  • Weekly scorecard: calls booked, content posted, experiments run, build tasks closed, rule-adherence %.
  • Run 10+ small experiments a month; expect ~30% to win.
  • Healthy unit economics target: LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1 and CAC payback under 6 months.

08 · GuardrailsHonest flags

Scraping and outreach rules. Respect each site's terms and robots rules (your own notes already show F6S blocks automated reads). Avoid scraping behind logins, especially LinkedIn. Collect only business contact data. Follow CAN-SPAM/GDPR for the US and EU targets, and check Zambia's data protection law for local lists. Always offer an opt-out.
Firstsource script claims. The mining doc says "we've built this for 3 other healthcare BPOs", quotes savings stats, and estimates a 60–75% close rate. Unless you have those case studies, remove or rewrite those lines. Invented proof burns trust and conflicts with KTMNGW's "no fake confidence". A large BPO also has a long procurement cycle, so treat it as a stretch account, not the first win.
Trading. The $5 → $20,480 challenge table is a mathematical doubling schedule, not a forecast. Doubling monthly for 12 months is extremely unlikely to hold. Publish it, if at all, as a process-and-discipline exercise with clear risk language, never as a promise.
Verify first: Octoparse plan limits, template availability for each target site, and export/API options. I only confirmed what their use-cases page states.
